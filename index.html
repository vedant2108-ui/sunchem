Who We Are

The Architecture of
Industrial Excellence

Founded with a singular mission — to elevate the quality of raw materials available to India's paint industry — Sunchem has grown into one of the most trusted names in chemical distribution and manufacturing. Our operations span three distinct verticals: in-house manufacturing, pan-India distribution, and direct import partnerships with global leaders.

We are the invisible infrastructure behind India's most recognised paint brands. Every litre of premium coating produced by our clients carries the precision, purity, and consistency that only comes from materials sourced and verified by specialists.

ISO-certified processes, a dedicated R&D team, and two decades of deep industry relationships make us the partner of choice for manufacturers who refuse to compromise.
